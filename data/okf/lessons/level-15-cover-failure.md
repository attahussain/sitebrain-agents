# What happened
Level 15 column rebar failed inspection: measured cover was 30 mm against the required 40 mm.[^report-13-oct]

# Rule introduced
Use 40 mm concrete spacer blocks at no more than 1 m centres on every column face, and have the
section foreman sign a cover checklist before calling for inspection.

See [Concrete cover](/specs/concrete-cover.md).
