# Question
Can concrete with a retarding admixture be discharged later than the 90-minute limit in Section 03300?

# Response
Yes. Mixes using the approved retarding admixture (supplier trial mix TM-07) may be discharged up to
120 minutes after batching, provided the temperature at discharge is still at or below 32 C and the
batch ticket records the admixture dose.[^rfi-021-letter] Mixes without the retarder keep the 90-minute limit.

Applies to: [Hot-weather concreting](/specs/hot-weather-concreting.md).
