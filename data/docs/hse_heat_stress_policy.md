# HSE Policy - Heat Stress Management (fictional demo project)

## Midday work ban
In line with the UAE midday break rule, outdoor work under direct sun is suspended between 12:30 and 15:00 from 15 June to 15 September.

## Hydration and rest
Cool drinking water and shaded rest areas must be provided within 50 m of every work front. Workers rest 10 minutes every hour when the heat index exceeds 40 C.

## Scaffolding
All scaffolds must carry a valid green inspection tag, re-inspected every 7 days and after high winds. Boards must be secured against displacement.

## Incident reporting
Near misses must be reported to the HSE officer within 24 hours and logged in the incident register.
