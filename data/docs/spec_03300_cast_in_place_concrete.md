# Specification Section 03300 - Cast-in-Place Concrete (fictional demo project)

## Concrete grades
Columns and core walls: C50. Slabs and beams: C40. Foundations and raft: C40 with sulphate-resisting cement.

## Concrete cover
Minimum cover to reinforcement: columns 40 mm, slabs 25 mm, beams 35 mm, foundations and raft 50 mm.

## Hot-weather concreting
Concrete temperature at the point of discharge shall not exceed 32 C. Use chilled water or ice where required.
Concrete shall be discharged within 90 minutes of batching; loads exceeding this time shall be rejected.

## Curing
Exposed surfaces shall be kept continuously moist for a minimum of 7 days using wet hessian or an approved curing compound.

## Testing
Six cube samples per 50 m3 per grade; 7-day and 28-day compressive strength tests.
