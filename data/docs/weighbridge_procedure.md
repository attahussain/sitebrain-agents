# Weighbridge Operating Procedure (fictional demo project)

Every delivery vehicle is weighed loaded on entry and empty on exit; net weight is the difference.
The weighbridge is calibrated every 6 months; permissible tolerance is plus or minus 0.5 percent.
A printed and digital ticket is issued for each movement and synced to the site SQL Server database.
Discrepancies above 0.5 percent between the supplier delivery note and the weighbridge ticket are escalated to the commercial team the same day.
